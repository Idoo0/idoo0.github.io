# Ridho Aditya

**Software · Artificial Intelligence · Data**

Hi, I'm Ridho Aditya. With a background in Computer Science at Sam Ratulangi University, I enjoy turning ideas into useful applications. My interests span software development, artificial intelligence, and data, from learning tools to applications that help people understand information and make decisions.

This portfolio brings together my projects, work experience, research, competitions, and community activities. Each project shares the problem it addresses, my contributions, and the outcomes.

## Featured Projects

- **NEUROKIT** — a learning toolkit combining a physical device, a companion app, AI, and interactive activities to support student attention. The project earned a Bronze Medal at PIMNAS 38, Indonesia's national student research competition.
- **Socratix** — a mathematics learning platform with an AI tutor and interactive visuals that help students understand each step toward a solution.
- **Hudaverse** — a web prototype for Quranic learning, with daily activities, progress tracking, and game elements.
- **CORA** — a competitive aptitude game featuring scientist characters and matches between players.
- **YUDHA** — a test preparation app for Indonesian civil service and state-owned enterprise recruitment, featuring quiz duels, progress tracking, and AI-assisted interview practice.
- **YukTanam** — my AI contribution to a gardening app, helping identify plant diseases from images and provide plant care information.

The portfolio also includes explorations in app development, data analysis, image recognition, and smart devices.
